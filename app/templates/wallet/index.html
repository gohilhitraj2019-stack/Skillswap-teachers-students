Platform Escrow Model
  • Direct Indian Rupee (₹) Pricing:
    All instructor hourly rates, packages, and bookings operate natively in Indian Rupees (₹). Eligible students can also book 1 Free Trial session per teacher.
  • Pre-Authorized Escrow Holding:
    When booking a paid class, the session fee is moved to Escrow Hold. If either party cancels before the session, 100% of the funds return instantly to your available balance.
  • Post-Session Settlement:
    Payment is settled only after the live session is marked complete, splitting the funds between the teacher's net earnings (80%) and platform fees (20%).
