How SkillSwap Works

Our escrow credit model protects both learners and mentors throughout the booking and completion lifecycle.

STEP 01
Select Instructor & Hold Credits

Browse verified mentors, check their open schedule, and select an hourly time slot. Required credits are held in escrow—never deducted until the class is complete.

STEP 02
Attend Live Video Class

Join the in-browser WebRTC classroom with crystal clear peer audio/video, screen sharing, live text chat, and a structured doubt-solving workspace.

STEP 03
Safe Settlement & Cash Out

When the class finishes, credits are automatically settled: the instructor receives their net earnings, and can withdraw directly to their bank account or UPI.
